What does this mean?

Score = ctr_arm + eng_arm. The CTR arm is exposure times the CTR gap below the page's position-tier median (pages with at least 500 impressions). The engagement arm is the engagement gap below the content-type-by-intent target, scaled by sessions (pages with at least 50 sessions).

Reason codes tell you the kind of fix: ctr_opportunity the page is visible but its CTR is below the tier norm, engagement_gap sessions are there but on-page engagement is weak, refresh_decay the page is old or stale.

This is decision support for a person. It ranks which pages to open; it never edits or publishes anything. See the paper's recommendations for the full archetype-to-action mapping.
